New lighting corrections affect newly generated mip data. Loom 1.16.1, Gradle 9.4.1, Sodium 0.8.11 and ARM64 runtime packaging remain.

The tester has confirmed the reported terrain-transition and Complementary water defects fixed in this setup; broader visual acceptance and performance measurements remain pending. Final `0.2.16-metal.1` is reserved for accepted reconciliation; current version is `0.2.16-metal.preview`.
